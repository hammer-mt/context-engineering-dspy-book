[detect.predict]
Classify whether a supplied passage is likely AI-generated or AI-rewritten according to the stylistic calibration below.

Input:
- `text`: the passage to classify.

Output:
- `reasoning`: A concise explanation grounded in specific wording, sentence structure, rhythm, transitions, repetition, and level of source-specific texture.
- `is_ai`: `True` if the passage is likely AI-generated or AI-rewritten; otherwise `False`.

Return only these two fields.

Evaluation method:
Judge the passage comparatively and holistically. Do not treat polished grammar, concision, technical terminology, or the mere presence of domain-specific names as decisive. Instead, determine whether the details retain locally authored irregularities or have been organized into globally smoothed, balanced, self-contained explanatory prose.

Evidence favoring `is_ai=True`:
- A carefully shaped progression that makes the passage fully self-contained for a general reader.
- Balanced contrasts or parallel clauses, such as simple versus complex, interface versus underlying system, or distributed versus complete.
- Parenthetical clarifications that neatly anticipate and resolve ambiguity.
- Redundant explanatory restatements of the same point in increasingly broad or accessible language.
- Tidy transitions and concluding clauses that repackage or summarize the preceding point.
- Uniform cadence, coordinated phrases, or polished pairings such as “unchanged in purpose and untouched in handling.”
- Slogan-like conclusions or abstract benefit language such as “more control, more flexibility.”
- Technical details arranged into a rhetorical pattern such as “field by field and type by type.”
- Source-specific terminology that remains present but appears to have been smoothed into a generalized rewrite.

Evidence favoring `is_ai=False`:
- Abrupt qualifications, direct commands, uneven cadence, minor awkwardness, or imprecise claims.
- Practical repetition that appears to come from teaching or documentation rather than rhetorical polishing.
- Locally specific implementation details, versioned names, unexplained technical terms, or idiosyncratic notation.
- Phrases such as `n>=2`, “without using for-loops,” or “might be of help.”
- Parentheticals, quotations, and terminology that feel inherited from a source rather than inserted to make the passage perfectly self-contained.
- Concrete lists or empirical details embedded in somewhat clumsy textbook prose.
- Repetition such as “server” several times in close proximity or awkward constructions such as “not from any other in the network.”

Important cautions:
- Technical specificity does not automatically imply human authorship. Ask how that specificity is presented.
- Awkwardness and source-local texture can outweigh otherwise grammatical prose.
- Conversely, a passage containing technical names can still be AI-rewritten if its overall construction is balanced, redundant, explanatory, and neatly concluded.
- Short passages are uncertain. Do not decide from one superficial feature.
- In `reasoning`, identify the actual decisive language. Do not merely call the prose “polished,” “formulaic,” or “technical.”
